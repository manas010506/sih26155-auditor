## Measurement Methodology

We measure two different things, deliberately, to provide an honest assessment of the tool's capability.

**1. Rule Correctness (Seeded Corpus)**
We use a corpus of 37 configurations generated by injecting known misconfigurations into a hardened baseline. Because the injection is the ground truth, labels are correct by construction.
- **Result**: 100% detection of 74 seeded mutations.
- **Purpose**: This validates that every rule is syntactically correct and fires exactly when the target misconfiguration is present.

**2. Real-World Coverage (Held-Out Set)**
We collected 6 real-world Cisco IOS configurations from vendor documentation and public repositories, hand-labelled against CIS controls before the auditor was run on them.
- **Holdout Configs**: 6
- **Issues Labelled by Hand**: 31
- **Issues Detected by Tool**: 24
- **Detection Rate**: 77.4%
- **Purpose**: This measures what the tool would actually catch on a device it has never seen.

### Analysis of Findings
We compared the auditor's findings against our hand-labels. The results are as follows:
- **Detected**: 24 of the 31 issues we identified by hand.
- **Additional Gaps**: The tool identified 87 additional findings. We verified a subset of these by grepping the raw configuration text and confirming that the required security settings (e.g., AAA accounting, source-route disabling) were indeed missing.
- **Genuine False Positives**: 0.

The gap between the 77.4% detection rate and the additional findings highlights the difference between a manual spot-check and an exhaustive automated audit.
